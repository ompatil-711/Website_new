# Photo shot list — Paradise Enterprise

Shoot with a phone. Same day if you can, so the light matches across all photos.

## Rules for every photo
- Daylight from a window or outdoors. No flash, no filters, no HDR mode.
- Hold the phone level (turn on the camera grid). Stand square to the board, not at an angle.
- Landscape unless noted. Use the normal 1x lens, not zoom. Wipe the lens first.
- Clean the board and the wall. Remove stickers, cables and other companies' names or logos from the frame.
- No people's faces unless they agreed (founders, installers).
- Send the original files (not WhatsApp-compressed): upload to a folder or email them.

## A. Products, on a plain wall (one board each) — 11 photos
Fill most of the frame with the board. Leave a little wall around it.
File names (these match the shop):
`whiteboard`, `greenboard`, `chalkboard`, `pinup-notice`, `combination`, `acrylic-cover`,
`magnetic`, `ceramic`, `key-board`, `drawing`, `digital`
Square crop is fine, but shoot landscape so I can crop.

## B. Close-ups of surfaces — 4 photos
Corner of the board, the frame edge, and the writing surface with a marker line on it.
`surface-ceramic`, `surface-magnetic` (with a magnet on it), `surface-green` (with chalk), `surface-cork`

## C. Installation — 3 photos
`install-mounting` (installer fitting a board, from the side, tall/portrait is fine),
`install-bracket` (close-up of the fixing/bracket),
`install-done` (the finished board on the wall, room visible)

## D. Finished rooms — 3 to 5 photos
Classrooms or offices you have equipped, taken from the doorway so the whole wall and some of the room show.
`room-1` … `room-5`

## E. Workshop and people — 3 photos
`workshop` (the factory floor or cutting/assembly in progress),
`founders` (portrait, daylight, in front of a board or the shopfront; only with their OK),
`shopfront` (the office or shop from the street, straight on)

## Total: about 25 photos. Minimum to start: all of A, plus `install-done`, `room-1`, `workshop`.

## Also send me (text)
1. Which numbers are true and OK to publish: years in business, number of institutions served, any other figure.
2. Names of institutions that agreed to be shown as clients.
3. Founders' names and one line each.
4. Delivery area, usual delivery time, warranty, whether prices include GST.
